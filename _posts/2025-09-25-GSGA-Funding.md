As graduate students, we know that sharing our research at conferences, workshops, and symposiums is a crucial part of our work. However, the costs of travel and registration can be a significant barrier, and finding funding can feel like a maze.
The good news? The **Graduate Student Government Association (GSGA)** offers travel funding specifically to help you present your work. 

**[Link to the Official GSGA Funding Page](https://www.augusta.edu/student-life/graduatesga/funding.php)**

---

## How much can be fund ?

_Student travel funding request reimbursement is as follows :_

| Your Situation                                                     | Reimbursement Amount |
| -----------------------------------------------------------------  | :------------------: |
| Attending an event (but not presenting)                            |       **$200** |
| Presenting (main speaker/author) **with** another funding source   |       **$300** |
| Presenting (main speaker/author) with **only** GSGA as your source |       **$500** |

>
It's important to note that the GSGA will prioritize students who do not have other sources of funding. You can receive up to **$500 per semester**, for a total of **$1000 per academic year**.
>

---

### Eligible Events

The fund is designed for travel to a "research event involving some peer-reviewed presentation". This includes:
* Conferences
* Poster Presentations
* Workshops
* Symposiums

The funding does **not** cover events like PhD summer schools.
